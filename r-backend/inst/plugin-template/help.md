# Coefficient of Variation

The standard deviation divided by the mean: spread relative to the size of the values, so variables in different units can be compared.

**Assumptions**
- Ratio-scale variable (a true zero) with a positive mean.

**Interpreting the output**
- A CV of 10% means the typical deviation from the mean is a tenth of the mean.

**References**
- Everitt, B. S., & Skrondal, A. (2010). *The Cambridge dictionary of statistics* (4th ed.). Cambridge University Press.
