# One Sample T-Test

Tests whether the mean of a continuous variable differs from a fixed test value.

**Assumptions**
- Observations are independent.
- The variable is roughly normal (less important for n > 30).

**Interpreting the output**
- *t*, *df* and *p* test whether the population mean equals the test value.
- The mean difference (mean − test value) and its confidence interval show the size and precision of the difference.
- Cohen's *d* = (mean − test value) / SD.
- The Wilcoxon signed-rank test is the rank-based alternative; its effect size is the rank-biserial correlation.

**References**
- Student (1908). The probable error of a mean. *Biometrika, 6*(1), 1–25.
