# Paired Samples T-Test

Compares two measurements taken on the same cases (e.g. before/after, two conditions), using the per-case differences.

**Assumptions**
- Pairs are independent of each other.
- The differences (Measure 1 − Measure 2) are roughly normal.

**Interpreting the output**
- *t*, *df* and *p* test whether the mean difference is zero.
- Cohen's *dz* = mean difference / SD of differences.
- The Wilcoxon signed-rank test is the rank-based alternative; its effect size is the rank-biserial correlation.

**References**
- Lakens, D. (2013). Calculating and reporting effect sizes to facilitate cumulative science. *Frontiers in Psychology, 4*, 863.
