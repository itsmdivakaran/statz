# Descriptive Statistics

Summarises continuous variables (centre, spread, shape) and tabulates categorical variables.

**Interpreting the output**
- Mean and SD describe roughly symmetric data; median and IQR are robust to skew and outliers.
- Skewness and kurtosis use the bias-corrected formulas (as in SPSS): values beyond about ±1 suggest notable non-normality.
- Shapiro-Wilk tests normality; with large samples even trivial departures are significant, so check plots too.

**References**
- Joanes, D. N., & Gill, C. A. (1998). Comparing measures of sample skewness and kurtosis. *The Statistician, 47*(1), 183–189.
